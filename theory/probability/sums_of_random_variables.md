## Sums of random variables


### Motivation: 

- Understanding the distribution of the sum of random variables is crucial in probability theory and statistics.
    - Example: In many real-world scenarios, we are interested in the total outcome of several random events. For instance, the total score of a player in a game over multiple rounds can be modeled as the sum of random variables representing the score in each round.
    - Another example is in finance, where the total return of a portfolio can be modeled as the sum of random variables representing the returns of individual assets.

- The question is: How can one determine the pdf of the sum of two or more random variables, given their individual pdfs?
